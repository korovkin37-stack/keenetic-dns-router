How lists work

A list is a ready-made set of IP networks of a service (for example youtube.com) published by the source. Three modes: networks and addresses are taken from the source as they are; the source's domains are resolved through your DNS; or both at once, merged. The result becomes routes in the policy's list table (500, 501...) towards the exit you chose.

"Update all" downloads fresh data for every list from the source, compares it with the stored one and applies the routes. It is the only thing that contacts the source. A list that lost more than half of its entries is held back until you confirm it in the list details.

Targets are checked before lists (see Routing order in Settings): where both cover the same address, the one with the lower pref wins.

curl is not installed: the lists cannot be downloaded.

What the header buttons do

×

Apply routes

Writes the routes into the tables from what is already stored: no DNS lookups, no downloads. Use it after changing an exit or the routing order, or when routes got lost (for example after a router reboot, if the hook is off).

Tasks

The task manager: what is running now step by step, the queue of waiting tasks (each can be taken out), and the history of the last tasks with their logs. A task started while another one runs waits in the queue.

Refresh everything

The whole cycle in one go: resolves the domains of all targets, downloads fresh data for every enabled list and applies the routes. It can take several minutes; it runs on the router, so the page can be closed.

Update & apply (Targets)

Resolves the domains of all targets again through the DNS servers from Settings, then applies the routes. Use it when the addresses of services may have changed.

Sync policies (Policies)

Reads the policies from Keenetic (names, marks, tables) into the panel. Use it after you created, changed or deleted a policy in Keenetic. Routes of a deleted policy are removed.

Update all (Lists)

Downloads fresh data for every list from the source and applies the routes itself.
